How to fix email mismatches

These attendance records couldn't be linked to students because:

  • Typos in attendance form: Student entered wrong email in Google Forms (e.g., "gmal.com" instead of "gmail.com")
  • Different email used: Student used a different email in the form than what's in Google Classroom
  • Student not enrolled: Student submitted attendance but isn't enrolled in any course

To fix: Either correct the email in the Google Sheets attendance data and re-sync, or update the student's email in Google Classroom.
